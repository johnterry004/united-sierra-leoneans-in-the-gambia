Mission Statement:

The mission of UNITED SIERRA LEONEANS IN THE GAMBIA union is to provide a mutually supportive and positive learning environment in which every individual member has the opportunity to develop oral communication and leadership skills, which in turn foster self-confidence and personal growth. And the development of our community like a general cleaning once in a month is a major priority as a union Buying some hygienic materials like plastic dust bin, hand glove, hand sanitizer, face mask etc.

Vision Statement:

To be the first-choice provider of dynamic, high-value, inspirational words, positivity motivational, experiential communication and leadership skills development in our community and homes. And also bring the good values of leaderships awareness of positive educational matters to the youth and young ones

Value Statement:

UNITED SIERRA LEONEANS IN THE GAMBIA core values are integrity, peace, love, perseverance to success dedication to excellence, service to the member, and respect for the individual. These are values worthy of a great organization, and we believe we should incorporate them as anchor points in every decision we make. Our core values provide us with a means of not only guiding but also evaluating our operations, our planning, and our vision for the future.

Our Activity

At United Sierra Leoneans in The Gambia (USLG),

we organize a wide range of activities designed to connect, empower, and inspire our community. Every event and program is created with the goal of strengthening unity, celebrating heritage, and supporting personal growth. Our key activities include: Cultural Celebrations & Heritage Festivals – keeping our traditions alive through music, dance, and art. Educational & Leadership Programs – workshops, seminars, and mentorship opportunities to empower members. Community Service & Volunteer Initiatives – neighborhood cleanups, health drives, and social support programs. Networking & Social Events – gatherings that foster friendship, collaboration, and professional growth. Youth & Family Programs – empowering the next generation through education, sports, and wellness activities. At USLG, we believe that every member can make a difference. By participating in our activities, you not only grow personally and professionally, but you also contribute to a stronger, united, and thriving community. Join us and be part of the change — together, we achieve more!
